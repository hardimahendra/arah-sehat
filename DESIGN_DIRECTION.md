# Arah Sehat — Design Direction

Primary style: Swiss / International. Supporting style: Humanist. Layout: clear staged information flow. Signature: the “care card” groups trust commitments, not a decorative health-tech illustration.

Palette source: ID 7 (`#004741`, `#F0EDE4`) and ID 73 (`#FF6B54`, `#14B8A6`), mapped directly to emerald, cream, coral, and teal. Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA. Avoid: diagnosis language, outcome claims, emergency advice, and generic glassmorphism.
